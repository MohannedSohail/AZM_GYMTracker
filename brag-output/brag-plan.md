# Brag Plan: AZM (عزم) Gym Tracker — 45s Master Showcase (60 FPS)

## What is this app?
AZM (عزم) is a high-contrast, offline-first Android gym workout tracker designed for workout partners sharing a single physical mobile device in the gym, featuring one-handed gym ergonomics (±2.5 kg, ±1 rep) and native Arabic RTL typography.

## The angle
"هاتف واحد. عزم يجمعكم."
A comprehensive, cinematic tour showing every screen and feature inside a realistic modern smartphone mockup with high-contrast emerald neon aesthetics, Arabic typography, and 60 FPS motion graphics.

## Target Specs
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Frame Rate: 60 FPS
- Duration: 45.0 seconds
- Layout: Split layout (Interactive phone mockup displaying live screens on one side + dynamic feature callouts and typography on the other)

## Storyboard (45 seconds):
1. **Scene 1 (0.0s - 4.5s) — Identity & Hook**:
   Glowing Ayn geometric logo, atmospheric radial aura, punchy hook: "تتمرنون سوا داخل الجيم؟ جهاز واحد يكفيكم بدون إنترنت وبدون حسابات".
2. **Scene 2 (4.5s - 9.0s) — Onboarding & First Trainee**:
   Phone appears displaying the onboarding slider (photographic artwork, smooth transition without bounce) and first member creation.
3. **Scene 3 (9.0s - 14.0s) — Participant Selection**:
   Phone displays "من يتمرن اليوم؟" with multi-member cards (Ahmed, Omar, Salman), color badges, selection counter, and "Start Workout" button.
4. **Scene 4 (14.0s - 19.0s) — Home Dashboard & Resume Hero**:
   Phone displays the branded top bar, recent sessions list, and the prominent "Resume In-Progress Workout" banner (zero data loss across app kill).
5. **Scene 5 (19.0s - 25.0s) — Active Session & Turn Rotation**:
   Phone displays the active workout session with the Turn Rotation carousel: "أحمد ✓" finishes set and turn automatically advances to "عمر".
6. **Scene 6 (25.0s - 30.5s) — One-Handed Gym Steppers & Rest Timer**:
   Phone displays the thumb-zone controls: tactile `±2.5 كغ` weight stepper and `±1` reps stepper, "✓ تم" button, and circular rest timer overlay (`01:30`).
7. **Scene 7 (30.5s - 35.5s) — Exercise Catalog & Muscle Groups**:
   Phone displays the categorized catalog: search bar, muscle filter chips (Chest, Back, Legs, Shoulders, Arms, Core) with bilingual names and custom exercise tags.
8. **Scene 8 (35.5s - 40.5s) — Workout History & Progress Analytics**:
   Phone displays chronological history cards with set breakdowns, followed by volume progression and weekly session charts.
9. **Scene 9 (40.5s - 45.0s) — Total Volume Ring & Dual Theme Reveal**:
   Phone displays the glowing emerald volume ring (`8,640 كجم`), personal record badges, fluid transition between Dark Mode and Light Mode, and official logo lock.
